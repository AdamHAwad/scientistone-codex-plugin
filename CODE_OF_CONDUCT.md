# Code of conduct

This project expects respectful, evidence-based work.

State uncertainty. Critique the work, not the person. Make technical disagreements specific enough to test. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, expose private information, or misrepresent another person's work.

Maintainers may edit or remove abuse and may limit participation to protect contributors and users. Report conduct concerns through the private contact path in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers will keep good-faith reports as private as practical.
